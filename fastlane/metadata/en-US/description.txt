SignUtils takes the busywork out of preparing signing assets for sideloading with an Apple Developer Program membership.

Instead of creating a certificate signing request, registering an App ID, ticking capabilities, picking device UDIDs, downloading a certificate, exporting a .p12 and generating a provisioning profile by hand, you answer a few questions and SignUtils does the rest through the App Store Connect API.

ONE GUIDED FLOW
• Choose Development or Ad Hoc signing and the certificate type
• Pick an existing App ID or register a new one, including wildcards
• Turn on the capabilities the app needs, such as App Groups, iCloud, Push Notifications, Associated Domains or Network Extensions
• Select registered devices or register new ones by UDID
• Create a new certificate or reuse one SignUtils made before

READY-TO-IMPORT ARCHIVES
Every bundle is a zip with the .p12, its password, the .mobileprovision, the certificate and a summary, ready for your signing tool of choice.

A LIBRARY YOU CAN TRUST
SignUtils keeps every bundle you generate, shows when it expires and notices when its certificate was revoked or its profile deleted. Share an archive again or copy its password anytime.

CLEAN UP JUST AS EASILY
Revoke certificates, delete provisioning profiles and App IDs, edit capabilities, and register, rename, enable or disable devices.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your API key and private keys stay in this device’s Keychain. SignUtils only talks to Apple’s App Store Connect API and has no accounts, analytics or servers of its own.

SignUtils requires an App Store Connect API key with the Admin or App Manager role. Explore the built-in demo to try every feature without one.

SignUtils is open source: github.com/itoolio/signutils
