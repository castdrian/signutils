# SignUtils Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

SignUtils does not collect, store or share any personal data with its developer or any third party.

## What stays on your device

- The App Store Connect API keys you add are stored in your device's Keychain.
- The private keys SignUtils generates for certificates are stored in your device's Keychain.
- The signing archives you generate are stored in the app's own container until you remove them.

## Who SignUtils talks to

SignUtils only connects to Apple's App Store Connect API at `api.appstoreconnect.apple.com`, using the API key you provide, to read and manage your own certificates, App IDs, devices and provisioning profiles. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

SignUtils has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Sharing

Archives leave the app only when you share or save them yourself through the system share sheet or save panel.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/itoolio/signutils/issues.
